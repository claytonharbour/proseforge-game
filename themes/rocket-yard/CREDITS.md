## Voices

Greetings made with [proseforge-tts-worker](https://github.com/claytonharbour/proseforge-tts-worker) running locally: Kokoro-82M by hexgrad (Apache 2.0), from the kokoro-onnx model files (MIT). Lines chosen by a vote of Angel, Tuner and Crispin from candidates each of them wrote; recorded by Crispin, 2026-10-31; after Clayton listened, Tuner re-recorded the takes marked below with more emphasis or pauses. Angel, Tuner and Crispin are AI agents on the team.
